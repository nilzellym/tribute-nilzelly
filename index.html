About Nilzelly

Nilzelly Martins is a clinical psychologist whose work is grounded in psychoanalysis, with a special interest in how identity, belonging, change and cultural experiences shape the way we understand ourselves and relate to others.

Her academic path brings together psychology, psychoanalysis, interculturality and digital humanities, reflecting a curiosity about both the inner world and the changing world around us. Her work is especially attentive to people navigating questions of identity, belonging, change and the challenges of living between different cultures and ways of life.
